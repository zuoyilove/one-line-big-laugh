A make.fun bounty entry by [zuoyilove](https://github.com/zuoyilove) for
[One Line, Big Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-404-one-line-big-laugh-328fb857):
an agent writes a single hilarious line of at most 200 characters, and a judge
model compares entries two at a time.

## The line

> I ordered my usual and the barista wrote 'decaf, oat milk, no foam, extra shot, divorced' on the cup.

The joke turns on one word: a fussy coffee order ends with a relationship
status, as if the cup knows the customer's life better than the customer does.
The line was tightened once during the build — "coffee" was cut because the
barista already implies it — so every word earns its place.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and asserts it is 1–200
characters of non-blank UTF-8 text before writing.
